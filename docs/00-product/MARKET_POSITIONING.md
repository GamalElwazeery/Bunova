# Market Positioning

## Category

Bunova is positioned as a **Café Management OS**, not a generic restaurant POS and not merely a cashier application.

The product is designed around the operational reality that cafés can combine counter sales, table service, production, gaming/time billing, Wi-Fi access, shisha, online ordering and multi-branch control in many different combinations.

## Competitive frame

The Egyptian market includes generic POS products, restaurant-focused suites, café-focused POS products, PlayStation/gaming café management tools, and Wi-Fi voucher/hotspot tools. Most solve one or two slices well; Bunova's opportunity is a coherent operational model across those slices without forcing a kiosk to configure restaurant complexity.

## Positioning pillars

1. **Capability-based, not one-size-fits-all** — kiosk, coffee shop, café-restaurant, traditional café and gaming venue use one product with different enabled capabilities.
2. **Unified bill** — products, service, timed gaming/room usage and Wi-Fi access can settle together.
3. **Venue-native** — sessions, resources and occupancy are first-class.
4. **Egypt-ready** — Arabic/RTL, local operational habits, ETA fiscal readiness and payment-provider adapters.
5. **Offline-resilient** — selling and allowed operational workflows continue through normal connectivity problems.
6. **Menuza-native integration** — customer menu/QR/online ordering is reused rather than rebuilt.
7. **Serious back office without cashier clutter** — simple operational surfaces, deep owner controls.

## Primary customer segments

- single-branch independent café;
- specialty coffee shop;
- café + bakery;
- traditional café/lounge;
- café + restaurant;
- gaming café / PlayStation lounge;
- multi-branch local chain (2–20+ branches);
- franchise-ready operators as a later maturity tier.

## Jobs-to-be-done

Owners need trustworthy sales/cash/stock/session visibility and less leakage.

Managers need one live picture of tables, stations, gaming, staff, devices and exceptions.

Cashiers need extremely fast checkout and modifier entry.

Waiters need table/session context without back-office clutter.

Baristas/cooks/shisha staff need a clear preparation queue.

Gaming supervisors need accurate timers/rates/device state and easy order attachment.

Customers need fast ordering, correct bill, optional QR/menu/Wi-Fi access and transparent loyalty value.

## Pricing strategy principles

Do not compete only on cheapest monthly price. Price by branch/capability tier and operational value, keep entry setup accessible to small cafés, and avoid charging in a way that punishes basic cashier concurrency. Hardware, fiscal support, premium integrations and advanced multi-branch/analytics may be monetized separately if customer value supports it.

Final price points require renewed competitor/pricing research immediately before commercial launch.
