# Product Vision

## Mission

Bunova is an Egypt-first, extensible Café Management OS that lets a one-counter coffee kiosk and a multi-floor café/restaurant/gaming venue operate from the same coherent platform without forcing either business to carry irrelevant complexity.

## Product thesis

A café is a venue of **orders + sessions + resources + production + access + people**, not merely a restaurant with fewer kitchen features.

Bunova therefore models five first-class operational truths:

1. **Commerce** — products, modifiers, bills, payments, refunds, shifts.
2. **Venue** — floors, tables, rooms, seats, occupancy and service sessions.
3. **Timed resources** — consoles, PCs, rooms, billiards or any resource billed by time.
4. **Production** — bar, cold bar, kitchen, dessert, shisha and other stations.
5. **Access** — Wi-Fi packages/vouchers and customer-access entitlements.

All five must be able to converge into one bill and one auditable operational history.

## Target business shapes

- coffee cart / truck;
- takeaway kiosk;
- drinks-only coffee shop;
- specialty coffee shop;
- traditional café;
- café + bakery;
- café + restaurant;
- gaming café / PlayStation lounge;
- internet café / coworking café;
- café + shisha lounge;
- multi-branch and franchise operators.

## Strategic differentiation

Bunova should win on:

- speed of high-frequency counter and table workflows;
- capability-based setup instead of one giant restaurant configuration;
- unified billing for products, time and access;
- excellent gaming/timed-resource and Wi-Fi operations as first-class domains;
- offline-resilient operations;
- Egyptian fiscal/payment realities;
- deep Menuza integration without duplicating customer-facing menu infrastructure;
- reusable F&B contracts with the Restaurant System without losing café-specific behavior;
- owner visibility into live operations, leakage, margins and venue utilization.

## Success outcomes

A successful production release must let an owner configure a branch from a preset, disable irrelevant modules, import/build the catalog, register devices/stations/resources, operate a full shift during weak connectivity, serve counter/table/online/gaming orders, reconcile money and stock, issue fiscal records, and review branch performance without parallel spreadsheets or shadow systems.

## Product quality bar

Bunova is not complete when every menu item exists. It is complete only when core journeys work end-to-end across success, permission, offline, failure, retry, cancellation, refund and reconciliation states with premium operational UX and trustworthy historical data.
