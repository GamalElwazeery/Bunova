## Existing authority topology

Bunova already had a deep native planning corpus before the kit arrived: root README/AGENTS/TODO, product and market authorities, architecture/domain maps, operational domain specifications, UX/design/localization standards, delivery/test/audit/security/launch strategies, governance/ADRs, contracts, rules, skills, specialists and workflows.

The adoption requirement is therefore integration and routing, not replacement.

## Product shape

Bunova is a multi-tenant Café Management OS and SaaS platform supporting compositions from kiosk/cart through table-service café, café+restaurant, gaming/internet café and multi-branch operations. Core risks include unified financial billing, timed resources, offline/replay/concurrency, inventory/recipes, gaming, Wi-Fi/MikroTik, production/KDS, Egyptian fiscal integration, Menuza interoperability, tenant/platform commercial entitlement separation and multi-surface Arabic/English premium UX.

## Planned technology frontier

P01 is planned to introduce Laravel/Livewire/Tailwind, PostgreSQL, Redis/Reverb/object storage and Flutter/Drift operational clients. Because those runtimes do not exist at M0, Laravel Boost/Dart MCP/runtime-specific test adapters are not considered configured merely by planning them.

## ExecutionKit reconciliation conclusions

1. Use exact pinned isolated kit runtime instead of destructive auto-install over accepted P00 history.
2. Keep `TODO.md` the only mutable task authority.
3. Use a deterministic, source-hash-bound `projection-json` adapter for machine lifecycle/dependency/routing semantics.
4. Register/rout Bunova-native agents, skills, rules, workflows and contracts directly through Agent OS.
5. Enable all seven capability systems; SEO is STANDARD and limited to Bunova public commercial acquisition surfaces.
6. State OS is required cross-cutting continuity and remains derived/advisory.
7. Premium Experience is REQUIRED because Bunova is strongly UI-bearing across web and Flutter operational surfaces.
8. Test/runtime adequacy is intentionally deferred until P01 creates actual product code; K00 still requires config/schema/integration/runtime-host validation.
9. Product implementation stays blocked until independent K00 audit and activation.
