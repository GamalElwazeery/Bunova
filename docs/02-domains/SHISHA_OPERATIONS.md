# Shisha Operations

## Purpose

Traditional cafés and lounges may treat shisha as a core product with preparation and recurring service, so Bunova models it explicitly without forcing all cafés to enable it.

## Catalog

Shisha can use standard product/variant/modifier primitives for type, flavor, mix, head/bowl, tobacco-free alternatives and paid extras. Complex mixes remain explainable on ticket/bill.

## Recipe and stock

Recipes may consume flavor/tobacco, coal and disposables. Coal refill policy may create tracked service consumption where operationally useful without overcomplicating simple venues.

## Production

Shisha routes to a configured production station and follows queued -> preparing -> ready -> served. Remake/problem flows record waste/reason.

## Service follow-ups

Table/session may raise shisha service requests such as coal refill or issue. Requests track response time and staff assignment but need not create a new charge unless configured.

## Compliance/configuration

Age/legal/venue restrictions vary by jurisdiction and must be configurable/documented before expansion beyond target markets. Bunova does not encode assumptions that would silently violate local requirements.
