# Product Surface Map

## Café Owner / Admin Web

Purpose: organization and branch configuration, multi-branch control, back office, analytics and exception resolution.

Primary areas: dashboard/control center, branches/devices/capabilities, catalog/pricing, floor/resources, production setup, inventory/procurement, staff/permissions/Payroll-Lite, gaming/resources/rates, Wi-Fi/router/packages, customers/loyalty/memberships/promotions, reservations, fiscal/reconciliation, reports, integrations, audit, settings and Bunova plan/usage summary.

## Bunova Platform Admin

Separate platform operations surface for tenant lifecycle and commercial service administration.

Primary areas: organizations, plan versions, trials/subscriptions, commercial entitlements, branch/device limit usage, SaaS payment/reconciliation status, service health, support cases, diagnostics, platform audit and commercial reporting. Tenant data access follows explicit platform permissions and is logged.

## Bunova Public Website

Arabic/English responsive product website covering capabilities, café archetypes, pricing/plan comparison, contact or signup/trial according to the go-to-market model, legal/privacy pages, help entry points and SEO/content surfaces. This is separate from Menuza customer menus.

## POS / Counter App

Touch-first high-frequency sales surface. Modes include normal and optional rush layout. Core areas: register/shift, product grid/search, cart/modifiers, customer, context/table/session attachment, payment/split tender, receipts/refunds, quick manager approval, sync/device status and entitlement/grace status when intervention is required.

## Waiter / Handheld Mode

My zone/tables, service requests, open table/session, current orders, add order, notes/modifiers, send to production, readiness notifications, bill request/split handoff. Must remain usable one-handed on common phones.

## Barista / Production Display

Station-specific queue, item detail/modifiers, timers, state transitions, holds/remakes/cancellations and handoff. Large touch targets, distance readability and sound/visual notification controls.

## Gaming Supervisor Mode

Resource grid, available/reserved/in-use/maintenance, start/pause/resume/transfer/end, players/rate/package, attached order, alerts and reconciliation of stale sessions.

## Customer Display

Cart/amount/tax/discount, payment status, pickup token, post-sale QR/loyalty/menu information, idle marketing content with privacy clearing between customers.

## Menuza

Separate product/customer surface integrated by contract: digital menu, QR-context ordering, online pickup/delivery and customer order status. Bunova does not duplicate Menuza's public experience.

## Captive Portal

Minimal Wi-Fi access surface: branding, voucher/token, terms/consent as required, entitlement status and link to Menuza. It must not become another independent menu CMS.

## Support / Diagnostics

Restricted surface or diagnostics mode for device identity, app version, sync queue, last server contact, printer/router/provider health, entitlement projection, log export/redaction and support bundle generation.
