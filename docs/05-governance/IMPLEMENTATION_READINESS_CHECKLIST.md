# Implementation Readiness Checklist

## Native planning readiness (P00)

P00 may close only when:

- product vision/scope/archetypes are stable;
- canonical glossary and capability model are agreed;
- Bunova Cloud Platform SaaS/tenant lifecycle and the separation of commercial entitlement from enabled capability are explicit;
- Platform Billing is explicitly separate from café customer Billing/Payments;
- domain map and ownership are closed;
- unified billing/time/session/offline architecture is accepted;
- Menuza/Restaurant/payment/fiscal/router boundaries are accepted;
- real-café operational finance boundaries such as supplier dues, controlled customer tabs and Payroll-Lite are explicitly scoped without pretending to be a general ledger/statutory payroll suite;
- phase plan and dependency order are consistent;
- UX/surface/design/localization standards include café operations, Café Owner/Admin, Bunova Platform Admin and the public Bunova commercial surface;
- security/test/audit/launch strategies are present;
- contracts/rules/skills/workflows/agents are present and mutually consistent;
- `TODO.md` contains executable deep tasks from repository initialization through production launch and SaaS commercialization;
- every TODO phase has authorities, acceptance/evidence intent and a gate;
- no critical plan uses unresolved `TBD` as a substitute for a task;
- planning cross-link/completeness audit finds no unresolved critical gap;
- AI-ExecutionKit remains intentionally absent from the native-planning snapshot.

Closing P00 means **native planning is complete**, not that product implementation may begin.

## Pre-implementation readiness (K00)

After P00, the dedicated K00 round must integrate the **current live AI-ExecutionKit** and its supported installer/systems without overwriting Bunova-native authority. K00 must prove deep registration/routing, single `TODO.md` backlog authority, system compatibility and integration validation, including Bunova-native Platform SaaS authorities.

**Only after both `P00-GATE` and `K00-GATE` are `[x]` may P01 product implementation begin.**
