# UX, Content and Localization Rules

- Arabic and RTL are first-class; English is equally supported.
- Operational surfaces optimize the operator's job, not database shape.
- Cover loading, empty, error, offline, denied, conflict, retry and destructive states.
- No fake production analytics/orders/customers/placeholders.
- Reuse semantic design components/tokens; avoid one-off styling drift.
- Touch targets and distance readability match device context.
- Never rely on color or sound alone for critical status.
- Errors state what happened and what the operator can do next.
- Financial/fiscal/irreversible actions clearly show impact before confirmation.
- New untranslated hard-coded user-facing strings are defects.
