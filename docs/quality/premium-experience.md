# Bunova Premium Experience Contract

Premium Experience is REQUIRED. It is a cross-cutting construction invariant, not a ninth OS.

Native design authority is `docs/03-experience/DESIGN_SYSTEM_AND_BRAND.md` plus `UX_QUALITY_STANDARD.md`, `SURFACE_MAP.md` and localization/accessibility/input rules. `executionkit.quality.json`, `executionkit.design.json` and `executionkit.components.json` make that authority machine-readable without replacing it.

All UI-bearing Bunova surfaces must use a canonical component/token system, extend before creating one-offs, and shrink legacy debt. Review requires production-shaped data and explicit loading/skeleton/empty/error/success/disabled/permission/degraded/offline/retry/conflict states where applicable; mobile/tablet/desktop; RTL/LTR; touch/mouse/keyboard/screen-reader; light/dark/system where supported.

Premium means operational speed and clarity as much as visual polish: POS tap count/focus/scanner behavior, one-handed waiter use, distance-readable production/gaming screens, accessible owner/platform administration, native-feeling Flutter behavior, responsive dense data and recoverable degraded/offline states. Generic CRUD, fake dashboard cards, decorative-only complexity and AI-template sameness fail review.
