# Bunova Content OS

Content OS is FULL and owns completeness/correctness/provenance/localization policy for human-facing content; it never owns product task state.

Scope includes Arabic-first and English UI copy, labels, validation/errors, offline/degraded/conflict messaging, receipts, fiscal explanations, Wi-Fi cards/captive text, onboarding/help, support/runbooks, notifications, public Bunova commercial content and accessible alternative text. Menuza-owned public-menu content remains behind the Menuza contract.

No pseudo-Latin filler text, generic AI filler, fake testimonials, fabricated metrics, unlicensed imagery or unexplained machine-generated production copy is acceptable. Arabic/English meaning and financial/fiscal terminology must remain equivalent. Content changes affecting public search surfaces coordinate with SEO OS; content rights/provenance remain Content OS responsibility.
