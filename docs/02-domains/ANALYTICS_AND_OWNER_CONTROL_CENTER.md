# Analytics and Owner Control Center

## Principle

Analytics is projection/read-model territory. Operational domains remain authoritative.

## Live Café control center

Branch live view should surface, subject to enabled capabilities:

- open/occupied/reserved tables and rooms;
- active gaming/timed sessions and available resources;
- bar/kitchen/shisha queue counts and oldest wait;
- pickup orders waiting;
- connected/issued Wi-Fi entitlement summary where supported;
- cashiers/staff on shift;
- critical device/router/station outage;
- unresolved payment/fiscal/sync exceptions.

## Commercial metrics

Revenue, net sales, tax, discounts, tips, refunds, average ticket, items/order, channel mix, payment mix, product/category/variant/modifier mix, branch comparison and daypart/hour trends.

## Margin/stock metrics

Theoretical COGS, gross margin, recipe cost, waste, stock variance, sell-through and low-stock risk.

## Service metrics

Queue/preparation/ready-to-serve times, table/session duration, service-request response, order throughput and abandonment/cancel/remake.

## Resource metrics

Gaming/resource utilization, revenue/hour, idle time, package usage, room utilization, reservation/no-show and maintenance downtime.

## Customer metrics

New/repeat, visit frequency, retention cohorts, loyalty participation/redemption, membership utilization and attach rate, always within privacy/consent policy.

## Leakage/risk signals

Shift variance, void/refund/discount override rate, complimentary consumption, unusual stock adjustments and open-session anomalies. Signals are explainable and reviewable, not automatic misconduct verdicts.

## AI-readiness

Later AI/insight features may summarize trusted metrics or detect anomalies only after definitions and data quality are stable. AI never becomes the only explanation for a financial/operational metric.
