# Skill: Café Operations UX

## Use when
Designing POS, waiter, production, gaming, venue, Wi-Fi or owner operational surfaces.

## Operator simulation

Before approving a flow, simulate the actual operator:

- cashier during rush with repeated common drinks and modifiers;
- waiter holding a phone one-handed among tables;
- barista reading a queue at distance;
- gaming supervisor watching many active timers;
- manager closing a shift with variance;
- owner comparing branches on mobile.

## Required design work

Map primary action, exceptional action, information priority, tap/keyboard count, offline/failure/conflict state, permission escalation, confirmation friction and recovery.

Use design-system components and semantic states; do not produce generic database forms/tables as the final operational UX.
